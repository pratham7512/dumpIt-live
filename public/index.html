Rambling is the point. A ten-minute brain dump becomes a summary, a few tasks, and a clean note. You don’t have to structure anything before you speak — that’s our job.

Your notes are yours. Voice notes are transcribed, then processed for organization. You can delete any dump, or your whole account, whenever you want. We don’t train public models on your dumps.
